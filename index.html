About个人简介

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology, advised by Prof. Junwei Zhou. My research centres on intelligent log analysis — making large-scale system logs tell us what went wrong, before the users notice.

我是武汉理工大学人工智能学院博士研究生，师从 周俊伟教授。 我的研究围绕智能日志分析展开：让大规模系统日志在用户察觉之前，把问题的原因说清楚。

Research Areas研究方向

Log Analysis & Anomaly Detection日志分析与异常检测 Unsupervised and cross-system detection, log parsing, template mining, sequence modelling on production log streams. 无监督与跨系统检测、日志解析与模板挖掘、生产日志流上的序列建模。
LLM for Systems (AIOps)大模型驱动的智能运维 Instruction-tuned models for anomaly typing and root-cause analysis; rule-engine + LLM cascades; cost-aware inference. 面向异常分类与根因分析的指令微调；规则引擎与大模型的级联架构；成本敏感的推理优化。
Data Systems Engineering数据系统工程 Database reliability, observability, and performance tuning. 数据库可靠性、可观测性与性能调优。
