I am a Master's student at the School of Artificial Intelligence,
[Wuhan University of Technology](https://www.whut.edu.cn/), advised by
Prof. [Junwei Zhou](https://junwzhou.github.io). 

My research focuses on AIOps and intelligent log analysis:

- **Cross-system log anomaly detection**: when and why unsupervised
  detectors fail to transfer across heterogeneous systems — failure
  boundary, underlying mechanisms, and how far limited labels can help;
- **Weak-label fault analysis**: using monitoring alerts (e.g., Zabbix)
  as weak labels together with kernel-level telemetry (Oracle ASH)
  for pre-failure signal mining.
